General Questions

1. What is RoopHub Official and how does it work?
RoopHub Official is a dedicated wellness and health information platform. Our mission is to research, analyze, and present the most effective health supplements and wellness products available in the market today. We act as a bridge between you and the official product manufacturers. When you decide to buy a product through our links, we redirect you to the secure, official website of the brand to ensure you get genuine products.
2. Is the information provided on this website medical advice?
No, the information provided on RoopHub Official is for educational and informational purposes only. While we take great care in researching the products we feature, we are not medical professionals or licensed doctors. The content on this site should not be used to diagnose, treat, or cure any disease. We strongly recommend consulting with a healthcare professional before starting any new dietary supplement.
3. Are the products mentioned on RoopHub sold directly by you?
No, RoopHub does not sell, manufacture, or ship any physical products directly. We are an independent affiliate partner. Our role is to provide information and direct you to the official source. When you click an "Order Now" or "Official Website" button, you are taken to the manufacturer's secure checkout page. This ensures that your payment is handled by a verified brand.
4. Do I have to pay extra if I buy through your affiliate links?
Absolutely not. Buying through an affiliate link on RoopHub does not cost you a single extra penny. In fact, it is often the opposite. Because we maintain professional relationships with these brands, we are sometimes able to offer our readers exclusive discount codes or special bundle pricing. The commission we earn is paid by the manufacturer as a marketing fee.

Product & Quality Questions

5. How do you choose which products to promote?
We take our reputation very seriously. We refuse to promote products that use deceptive marketing, fake testimonials, or hidden ingredients. Our selection process involves checking the purity of ingredients, the reputation of the manufacturing facility (such as FDA-registered or GMP-certified facilities), and real customer feedback. If a product does not meet our high standards, it will not be featured on our platform.
6. Can I get a refund if the product doesn't work for me?
Since the actual purchase happens on the official manufacturer's website, their specific refund policy will apply. Most high-quality supplements we feature come with a 60-day or 90-day money-back guarantee. We always encourage our users to read the "Refund Policy" on the official product page before completing a purchase. If you need help finding where to contact their support team, you can reach out to us.

We love hearing from our community! Whether you have a question about a specific review, want to suggest a product for us to analyze, or have a technical issue with our website, you can reach out to us through our official Contact Us page.
