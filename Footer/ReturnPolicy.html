Last Updated: April 17, 2026

At RoopHub Official, we are committed to ensuring your satisfaction with the products and services we promote. Please read this policy carefully to understand your rights and our procedures regarding returns and refunds.

Important Notice

RoopHub Official is an affiliate marketing platform. We do not sell, manufacture, or ship products directly. All purchases are made through the official manufacturers' websites, and their specific return and refund policies apply.

1. Purchases Made Through Our Links

When you click on a product link on RoopHub and make a purchase, you are buying directly from the official manufacturer's website. This means:

  • Your transaction is processed by the manufacturer, not RoopHub.
  • The manufacturer's return, refund, and warranty policies apply to your purchase.
  • Any customer support issues should be directed to the manufacturer.
  • RoopHub earns a commission from qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you.

2. Money-Back Guarantees

Many of the high-quality supplements we feature come with manufacturer-backed money-back guarantees. These typically range from 60 to 90 days from the date of purchase. However, the specific terms vary by product and manufacturer.

We always encourage our users to read the "Refund Policy" or "Terms of Sale" on the official product page before completing a purchase. If you need help finding where to contact their support team, you can reach out to us.

3. How to Request a Refund

Since purchases are made on the manufacturer's website, you must contact them directly for refund requests. Here's the general process:

  • Locate your order confirmation email from the manufacturer.
  • Visit the manufacturer's official website and find their "Contact Us" or "Support" page.
  • Provide your order details and reason for the refund request.
  • Follow the manufacturer's instructions for returning the product (if required).
  • Allow the specified processing time for your refund (typically 5-10 business days).

4. Shipping and Delivery Issues

Any issues related to shipping, delivery delays, lost packages, or damaged products must be addressed directly with the product manufacturer's customer support team. They are responsible for fulfilling and delivering your order.

5. Non-Returnable Items

Certain items may be non-returnable depending on the manufacturer's policy. These often include:

  • Opened or used health supplements (due to hygiene and safety regulations).
  • Products past their expiration date.
  • Items without original packaging or proof of purchase.
  • Products purchased from unauthorized sellers.

6. How We Can Help

While RoopHub cannot process refunds or handle returns directly, we are here to assist you in other ways:

  • We can help you locate the official manufacturer's contact information.
  • We can provide guidance on navigating the refund process.
  • If you encounter issues with a manufacturer, we can escalate concerns on your behalf.
  • We take feedback seriously and may remove products from our platform if there are consistent issues.

7. Consumer Rights

Regardless of the manufacturer's policies, you have certain consumer rights that are protected by law. These may include the right to receive products as described, the right to a refund for undelivered items, and protection against fraudulent charges. If you believe your consumer rights have been violated, please contact us and we will do our best to assist you.
