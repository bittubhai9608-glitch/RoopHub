Community Guidelines and Safety Information

Important Guidelines

To maintain a safe and trustworthy community, please review and follow these important guidelines when using RoopHub Official.

Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on RoopHub Official and may result in immediate action:

Don't Share Personal Information

Never share credit card numbers, passwords, or SSN. We'll never ask via email or message. Protect your sensitive data at all times.

Don't Post Spam

Avoid external links and repetitive promotional messages. Spam undermines the integrity of our platform and wastes everyone's time.

No False Claims

All content must be genuine and based on actual experience. Misleading information can harm other users and damage trust in our community.

Don't Engage in Harassment or Bullying

We maintain a Zero Tolerance policy for harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks. Treat all community members with respect and dignity.

Don't Attempt to Hack or Manipulate

Security is non-negotiable. Never attempt unauthorized access, DDoS attacks, or any form of cyber-attack against our platform.

Don't Sell Illegal Items

Do not attempt to sell counterfeit goods, prohibited items, or engage in any illegal commerce through our platform.

Enforcement Policy

Enforcement Notice

Violations of these guidelines will result in account suspension or permanent ban. We reserve the right to take any necessary action to protect our community and platform integrity.

Positive Community Practices

To help us maintain RoopHub as a trusted space, we encourage you to:

Be Respectful: Treat all community members with courtesy and respect.

Share Honestly: Provide genuine feedback and authentic experiences.

Report Issues: If you see something suspicious or problematic, report it to us immediately.

Stay Informed: Read our Terms, Privacy Policy, and other guidelines to understand your rights and responsibilities.

Need Help or Have Questions?

If you have any questions about these guidelines or need assistance with any issues, please don't hesitate to contact our support team. We're here to help ensure a safe and positive experience for everyone.

Thank you for helping us keep RoopHub a trusted space!
